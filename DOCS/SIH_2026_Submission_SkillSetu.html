4. Abstract / Summary

Word / Character Compliant (Max 10,000 characters)

SkillSetu – AYUSH Career Bridge is an intelligent, competency-first digital collaboration platform engineered to bridge the structural divide between academic education and industry expectations. Submitted for Smart India Hackathon 2026 under Problem Statement ID: 26044 (Ministry of Ayush / All India Institute of Ayurveda), the platform directly targets the curriculum-skill latency gap that leaves students unprepared for practical employment and forces recruiters to sift through unverified, keyword-stuffed resumes.

Traditional recruitment portals function as passive advertising directories that treat employment as a disconnected transaction. SkillSetu replaces this outdated paradigm with an active, tripartite ecosystem connecting Students, Academic Institutions, and Industry Employers through a continuous six-stage progression lifecycle:

LEARN  ⟶  ASSESS  ⟶  VERIFY  ⟶  MATCH  ⟶  APPLY  ⟶  GROW

Key System Innovations & Workflows:

  • Three-Tier Skill Taxonomy & Dynamic Gap Analysis: Student competencies are structured into Basic (syntax & concepts), Intermediate (tooling & APIs), and Advanced (system design & optimization) tiers. When targeting aspirational roles, students receive an immediate visual diagnosis of missing competencies alongside step-by-step modular learning roadmaps.
  • Objective 100-Point Match Engine: Candidate-to-opportunity matching is governed by a transparent, multi-factor scoring rubric: Skill Overlap (60%), Verified Assessment Audit (15%), Practical Project Portfolio (10%), Prior Sprint Experience (5%), Academic CGPA (5%), and Proximity Radius (5%), completely eliminating superficial resume keyword screening.
  • Tripartite Stakeholder Collaboration:
    • Students gain transparent career progression, verified skill credentials, and real-time application status tracking.
    • Academia / Colleges obtain batch-wide skill readiness heatmaps, automated placement eligibility thresholds, and tools to modernize curricula via bilateral industry MoUs.
    • Industry gains precision skill-first talent search, an 8-stage interactive Kanban recruitment pipeline, code benchmark evaluation, and standardized digital offer generation.
  • Location-Based Opportunity Radar: Utilizing the Browser Geolocation API and OpenStreetMap/Leaflet, students dynamically discover nearby startups, institutional internships, and industry partners across customizable radius boundaries (5 km to 100 km).

Technical Implementation Status:

The operational prototype is built with React, Next.js, TypeScript (strict mode), and Tailwind CSS, featuring role-specific dashboards, client-side execution of the 100-point matching algorithm, interactive Leaflet geo-mapping, and an interactive Kanban hiring board. The production roadmap outlines an enterprise Node.js/PostgreSQL microservices backend, secure role-based access control (RBAC), proctored assessment sandboxes, and proposed AI/ML models for automated syllabus gap discovery and semantic skill-to-role vector matching.

By transforming recruitment from static resume claims into verified, demonstrated competencies, SkillSetu provides a scalable, national-level solution ensuring no student is left behind due to lack of career direction or industry misalignment.

5. Idea Description

Comprehensive SIH Narrative (Max 50,000 characters)

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

In higher and technical education across India—including technical, bio-computational, healthcare, and allied domains under the purview of institutions like the All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA) and the Ministry of Ayush—a critical structural divide persists: the Curriculum-Skill Latency Gap. Academic curricula update on multi-year institutional revision cycles, whereas industry, high-growth startups, and modern research organizations evolve their required skillsets on a continuous basis.

This disconnect produces three systemic bottlenecks:

  1. The Student Dilemma: Students graduate with degrees and theoretical coursework but lack a quantitative understanding of their actual practical competencies, current market expectations, or specific skill gaps. Traditional job search engines merely index static resumes that reward keyword stuffing over demonstrated practical competence.
  2. The Industry Hiring Bottleneck: Employers and startups face overwhelming volumes of unvetted resumes. Without standardized, capability-based validation, recruiters spend hundreds of hours filtering candidates through superficial proxies (such as college tier or CGPA) rather than verifiable skill competencies.
  3. The Academia Visibility Void: Academic institutions and placement cells lack dynamic, data-driven visibility into the real-time skill distribution of their student cohorts and emerging industry demands. Consequently, curriculum enhancements, bilateral Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs), and campus training interventions remain reactive and anecdotal.
SIH Problem Alignment: SkillSetu is engineered specifically to address Problem Statement ID: 26044 ("Portal for Academia - Industry collaboration for Skill Mapping, Internships and Placement"), transforming recruitment from a passive job board into an active competency-first collaboration bridge.

2. Core Differentiator: The Six-Stage Continuous Competency Loop

Unlike traditional job portals that simply list job vacancies, SkillSetu operates on a structured, closed-loop progression pipeline:

  • LEARN: Students access structured learning modules and curated technical resources tailored to specific industry competencies.
  • ASSESS: Students undergo progressive, modular competency evaluations to benchmark their conceptual and practical grasp.
  • VERIFY: Successful evaluations yield verified digital skill credentials and competency ratings, moving talent evaluation from self-proclaimed resume claims to objective, demonstrated capability.
  • MATCH: A transparent, multi-dimensional match engine evaluates candidate skill profiles directly against verified industry role criteria, calculating an objective match score.
  • APPLY: Students discover and apply directly to matching internships, apprenticeships, micro-sprints, and full-time opportunities with complete pipeline transparency.
  • GROW: Continuous feedback from assessments and recruiter requirements reveals actionable skill gaps, recommending the next step in the student's career trajectory.

A. Student Workflow

  • Profile & Taxonomy Mapping: Students build a dynamic competency profile categorizing their capabilities across a three-tier taxonomy: Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced.
  • Skill Gap Identification: When a student targets an aspirational role (e.g., Data Analyst, Python Developer, Health-Informatics Associate), the platform dynamically compares their current verified skills against the target role's prerequisite matrix, visually highlighting exact skill deficits.
  • Targeted Learning & Assessment: Students access structured learning roadmaps addressing their identified deficiencies and complete modular quizzes to validate progression.
  • Location-Based Discovery: Utilizing browser geolocation and interactive maps, students discover regional startups, institutional internships, and industry partners within custom distance radii (5 km to 100 km).
  • Application Lifecycle Tracking: Real-time status tracking across screening, technical interviews, and formal offer letter issuance eliminates recruitment opacity.

B. College & Academia Workflow

  • Batch-Wide Competency Heatmaps: Institutional dashboards aggregate department-level skill proficiencies across cohorts, providing placement officers with an empirical baseline of student capability.
  • Curriculum Alignment & Gap Analytics: By aggregating recruiter requirements and candidate assessment results, colleges gain actionable intelligence on syllabus blind spots, enabling data-backed curriculum revisions.
  • Placement Drive & Eligibility Automation: Placement cells define granular eligibility criteria (combining academic CGPA with mandatory verified skill thresholds), preventing mismatched candidate submissions.
  • Institutional Industry Partnerships: Digital management of bilateral Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs), industry-sponsored hackathons, and guest faculty immersion initiatives.

C. Industry & Corporate Workflow

  • Skill-First Candidate Discovery: Employers filter applicants directly by verified skill competencies, sub-discipline proficiencies, and project validation rather than static resume text.
  • Transparent Multi-Factor Matching: Evaluates candidates against specific job role requirements through an objective, weighted scoring framework.
  • 8-Stage Hiring Pipeline: An interactive Kanban pipeline (New Application → Screening → Shortlisted → Technical Assessment → Technical Interview → HR Interview → Selected → Formal Offer Sent) that streamlines communication and reduces hiring turnaround time.
  • Academic Collaboration & Curriculum Studio: Industry partners submit advisory inputs to academic senates regarding obsolete tools or emerging framework requirements, directly bridging curriculum latency.
  • Digital Offer Generation: Standardized generation of structured internship and employment offer packages directly within the platform.

4. Skill Mapping & Assessment Engine

A. Granular Three-Tier Taxonomy

  • Basic Level (Knowledge & Syntax): Core principles, theoretical understanding, language syntax, and basic operational workflows.
  • Intermediate Level (Application & Tooling): API integration, database management, testing methodologies, and standard industry workflows.
  • Advanced Level (System Design & Optimization): Architectural design, scalability, security hardening, automated pipelines, and edge-case resolution.

B. Transparent 100-Point Match Algorithm

To eliminate hiring bias and subjective screening, SkillSetu evaluates candidate fit using an objective, multi-dimensional scoring rubric:

Evaluation Component Weight Operational Metric
Skill Overlap 60% Direct intersection between candidate verified skills and job mandatory skills
Verified Assessment Audit 15% Standardized score achieved in platform competency evaluations
Project & Practical Portfolio 10% Validated repositories, capstone projects, and code artifacts
Prior Sprint / Internship Experience 5% Previous practical exposure and verified micro-sprint completion
Academic Baseline (CGPA) 5% Institutional academic record and foundational coursework performance
Proximity & Work-Mode Alignment 5% Geographic match for hybrid/on-site roles or remote-readiness

5. Location-Based Opportunity Discovery

Regional collaboration is vital for micro-internships, local startup incubation, and specialized research centers (such as state Ayush research institutes and regional testing laboratories).

  • Browser Geolocation API: Safely captures user-consented browser coordinates to center the opportunity radar.
  • Interactive Mapping (Leaflet & OpenStreetMap): Renders interactive map views displaying verified corporate offices, research facilities, and colleges without proprietary API licensing bottlenecks.
  • Proximity Radius Filtering: Enables students and recruiters to adjust search boundaries (5 km, 15 km, 25 km, 50 km, 100 km) to find localized apprenticeships, summer internships, and physical campus drives.

6. Current Prototype Capabilities vs. Proposed Production Architecture

To maintain strict technical credibility and transparency for SIH evaluators, the platform architecture is cleanly separated into current operational capabilities and planned production enhancements:

A. Current Prototype Capabilities (Fully Functional in Current Build)

  • Frontend Architecture: Built using a modern component architecture with React, Next.js, and TypeScript in strict mode, styled with Tailwind CSS.
  • Role-Based Routing & Dashboards: Dedicated operational portals for Students, Colleges, and Industry with dedicated authentication flows and role-specific KPI dashboards.
  • Interactive Leaflet & OpenStreetMap Integration: Live map view rendering institutional coordinates, startup headquarters, and dynamic radius circles using browser geolocation.
  • Deterministic 100-Point Matching Engine: Client-side execution of the multi-factor candidate ranking algorithm based on active opportunity parameters.
  • Interactive 8-Stage Kanban Board: Drag-and-drop recruitment workflow management with state synchronization.
  • Client-Side State Persistence: Persistent session states using React Context and browser storage engines to showcase uninterrupted user flows during demonstrations.
  • Curriculum Advisory & MoU Exporter: Institutional agreement generator producing standardized collaboration documents.

B. Proposed / Planned Production Capabilities (Scalability Roadmap)

  • Production Backend & Database Layer: Transition to a secure Node.js/FastAPI REST/GraphQL microservices architecture backed by PostgreSQL for relational transactional data and Redis for session caching.
  • Secure Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Enterprise authentication featuring JWT tokens, OAuth2, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) compliant with data protection standards.
  • AI/ML Skill Gap & Career Recommendation Engine (Proposed):
    • Natural Language Processing (NLP) models to parse emerging industry job descriptions and extract trending competency vectors.
    • Semantic vector matching (using cosine similarity on skill embeddings) to calculate non-linear career recommendations.
    • Automated curriculum comparison comparing university syllabi text against real-time industry requirement clusters.
  • Proctored Assessment & Live Code Sandbox: In-browser coding and testing sandboxes with automated test case evaluation.
  • National & Enterprise Registry Integration: Secure API connectors for Digilocker, Academic Bank of Credits (ABC), and National Career Service (NCS) frameworks.

7. How SkillSetu Solves Problem Statement 26044

  1. Solves Information Asymmetry: Replaces vague resumes and generic job postings with standardized, level-indexed skill requirements.
  2. Eliminates Recruiter Screening Fatigue: The 100-point transparent match score immediately bubbles up the most capable candidates, cutting time-to-hire by an estimated 40–60%.
  3. Empowers Academic Governance: Colleges no longer operate blind; they receive empirical analytics on which subject areas fail to meet industry thresholds.
  4. Fosters Regional & SME Inclusivity: Startups and regional research centers that cannot afford expensive corporate recruitment portals gain direct, map-based access to local, verified college talent.

8. Scalability, Security & Future Scope

  • Horizontal Disciplinary Scaling: While demonstrated on core engineering and digital competencies, the underlying three-tier taxonomy is domain-agnostic. It can scale directly to specialized disciplines including Ayurvedic Pharmaceutical Manufacturing, Herbal Quality Control, Clinical Research, and Bio-Medical Informatics.
  • Privacy by Design: Candidate location queries operate strictly with client-level permission; recruiter views display candidate capability data without exposing private student contact details prior to formal shortlisting.
  • National Policy Alignment: Designed to support the objectives of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and AICTE internship mandates by digitizing industry-academia bilateral MoUs and credit-aligned apprenticeship tracking.
